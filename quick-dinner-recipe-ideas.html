Quick Dinner Ideas for Busy Evenings

When time is short, choose a meal built around ingredients that cook quickly or are already prepared. A simple plan is often more useful than a complicated recipe with many steps.

Try AI Cook ↗Browse recipes

Ideas to get started

Poha can work for a light evening meal, while dal and rice or a quick paneer curry can make a more filling dinner. Use leftovers safely and reheat them thoroughly rather than leaving food at room temperature for long periods.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

Tell AI Cook how much time you have and list ingredients that need using first. Check whether the suggested preparation and cooking times fit your schedule.

How to use Cookipie
