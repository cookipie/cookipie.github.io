AI Recipe Generator: Cook with What You Have

Not sure what to cook with the food already in your kitchen? Start with the ingredients you have, choose a meal and dietary preference, and use Cookipie’s AI Cook to explore a recipe idea instead of shopping for a long list of extras.

Ideas to get started

Try combinations such as potatoes and peas, rice and lentils, paneer with tomatoes, or leftover vegetables with spices. Results depend on the ingredients and options you enter, so review every suggestion and adjust it to your kitchen.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

List the main ingredients first, then mention useful extras such as oil, salt, cumin, turmeric, garlic or lemon. Add allergies and dietary restrictions clearly; always verify the generated recipe before cooking.

How to use Cookipie

  1. List the ingredients you actually have and note any dietary restrictions.
  2. Choose a meal or cooking preference in AI Cook.
  3. Read the full ingredient list and method before cooking; adapt the suggestion to your equipment and experience.
