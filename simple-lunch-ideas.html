Simple Lunch Ideas for Everyday Meals

A practical lunch can be built around one main dish and a simple accompaniment. Consider what is already cooked, how much time remains and whether the meal needs to travel.

Try AI Cook ↗Browse recipes

Ideas to get started

Dal with rice, chole with roti, paneer curry with rice or paratha with yoghurt are flexible ideas. If packing lunch, use a clean insulated container and keep foods at safe temperatures.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

Tell AI Cook how many people you are serving and whether you need a lunchbox-friendly dish. Scale quantities and cooking times carefully instead of assuming they change linearly.

How to use Cookipie

  1. List the ingredients you actually have and note any dietary restrictions.
  2. Choose a meal or cooking preference in AI Cook.
  3. Read the full ingredient list and method before cooking; adapt the suggestion to your equipment and experience.
