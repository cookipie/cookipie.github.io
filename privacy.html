Privacy Policy

Information this site processes

Public recipe browsing does not require an account. If you sign in with Google, the authentication provider returns account details such as a user identifier, name and email address. Cookipie uses the account identifier to associate saved recipes, likes and other account features with you.

Saved recipes, likes and reviews

When you use account features, the site may store recipe IDs, AI-generated recipes you choose to save, recipe likes, review text, ratings and comments. Reviews and comments may be visible publicly with the display name used by the site. Do not post private or sensitive information in public review fields.

AI Cook

If you use AI Cook, the ingredients and selected meal/diet preferences you submit are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Do not enter personal, confidential or sensitive information in the ingredient field. AI-generated results may be inaccurate.

Service providers and storage

Cookipie uses third-party services for hosting, authentication, database/API operations and AI generation, including GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Supabase/Google sign-in and Google Gemini when configured. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses browser local storage for preferences such as language and may use it for locally saved recipes. Authentication providers may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sign-in sessions.

Your choices

You can avoid signing in, avoid using AI Cook, and avoid posting public reviews. To request help with account data or privacy questions, a direct contact channel must be provided by the site owner; until then, do not submit sensitive information through the site.
