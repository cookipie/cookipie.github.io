What Can You Make with Tomatoes?

Tomatoes add acidity and body to many dishes. They can form a curry base, enrich lentils or become a quick sauce with suitable spices and aromatics.

Try AI Cook ↗Browse recipes

Ideas to get started

Tomatoes pair well with paneer, chickpeas and lentils. Cook them until softened and the raw smell fades when a recipe calls for a cooked gravy base; timing depends on the quantity and pan.

Tips for your kitchen

Tell AI Cook which other ingredients you have, especially onion, ginger, garlic, dairy or pulses. If you want a no-onion or no-garlic dish, state that clearly.

Frequently asked question

Can tomatoes replace every curry ingredient?

No. Tomatoes provide acidity and body but cannot replace all spices, aromatics or other ingredients. Review the recipe and adapt it to what you have.
