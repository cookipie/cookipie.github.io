Good food, made easier

Cookipie is a recipe website for home cooks who want practical ideas, clear instructions and familiar Indian flavours. Recipes are presented in English and Hindi so more people can follow along comfortably.

What you can do

  • Browse vegetarian and non-vegetarian recipes.
  • Search by recipe name, ingredient or category.
  • Save favourites to your account and share recipes.
  • Use AI Cook for recipe ideas based on ingredients you enter, when the service is available.

We aim to make cooking inspiration welcoming and useful. Always use your own judgement about allergies, dietary needs and safe food handling.
