No Onion, No Garlic Recipe Ideas

Many Indian dishes can be adapted without onion and garlic. Flavour can come from cumin, mustard seeds, ginger, tomatoes, hing where suitable, fresh herbs and other spices.

Try AI Cook ↗Browse recipes

Ideas to get started

A simple dal tadka, potato curry or tomato-based paneer dish can be prepared without onion or garlic by adjusting the tempering and gravy method. Not every recipe works the same way, so check each step.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

State “no onion and no garlic” explicitly in AI Cook and review the ingredient list before cooking. If the restriction is due to allergy rather than preference, check packaged spice blends and cross-contact risks too.

How to use Cookipie

  1. List the ingredients you actually have and note any dietary restrictions.
  2. Choose a meal or cooking preference in AI Cook.
  3. Read the full ingredient list and method before cooking; adapt the suggestion to your equipment and experience.
