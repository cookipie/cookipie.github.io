Easy Paneer Dinner Ideas

Paneer makes a flexible centrepiece for a vegetarian dinner. For a rich meal, use a curry base; for a quicker dish, choose a simpler masala or sautéed preparation with vegetables.

Ideas to get started

Paneer butter masala and shahi paneer are popular gravy options. If you are short on time, ask AI Cook for a faster version and mention which ingredients you do not have.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

Cut paneer into evenly sized pieces and add it near the end of cooking. If the paneer is firm, a brief soak in warm water may soften it before use.

Frequently asked question

How do I keep paneer from becoming rubbery?

Avoid simmering paneer for too long. Add it near the end and heat gently until warmed through.
