Rice and Dal: Simple Meal Ideas

Rice and lentils are pantry staples that can become a comforting everyday meal. The final texture and cooking method depend on the type of lentil, rice and equipment you use.

Try AI Cook ↗Browse recipes

Ideas to get started

A bowl of dal with rice is a simple starting point. Add a separate tempering with cumin, garlic or dried chilli if these suit your preferences. Vegetables can be cooked into the dal or served alongside it.

Make the suggestion work for your kitchen

Rinse lentils and rice as appropriate, use the water ratio for the specific variety, and allow pressure cookers to depressurize according to their instructions. Avoid forcing a pressure cooker open.

Frequently asked question

Can different lentils use the same cooking time?

No. Lentil varieties and whether they were soaked affect cooking time. Follow a recipe designed for the type you are using and check that they are fully tender.
