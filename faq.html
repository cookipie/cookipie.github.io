Frequently Asked Questions

Are Cookipie recipes free to read?

Yes. You can browse and read public recipes without signing in.

Can I switch between Hindi and English?

Use the Hindi / EN button on pages that show the language control. Some recipe names and ingredient labels may still use both languages together.

Do saved recipes sync across devices?

Sign in to use account-based saving. Recipes saved before signing in may be stored only in your current browser until account sync succeeds. Avoid relying on a single device copy as a backup.

How does AI Cook work?

Enter ingredients and choose a meal and dietary preference. If the service is configured and available, it sends the request to an AI provider to generate a bilingual recipe. Check the generated recipe carefully before cooking.

Can I rely on recipes for allergies or medical diets?

No recipe website can guarantee suitability for every allergy or medical condition. Check ingredient labels and consult a qualified professional when needed.
